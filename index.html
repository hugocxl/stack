CXL Design System Guidelines

CXL Design System

CXL is the design system we use across our websites. It brings the writing, drawings, and controls together in one place.

Move across the keys to try the keyboard, or use the arrows to explore another figure. Each drawing responds in place, keeping the example close to the writing.

Interface guidelines

Start with what the reader came for. Explain the subject in a few connected sentences, then bring in an example where it answers a question. On a product screen, name the object being edited and keep the action beside it. In an article, a sentence can lead straight into the next thought.

Keep prose within a centered column of up to 60ch. Let illustrations reach the page gutters when the details need more room. Use about a line of space between paragraphs, with a longer pause when the subject changes.

Type and color

Set the page in Geist Sans. Use weight 300 for reading, 400 for controls, and 600 for headings that need emphasis. At 1440 px wide, body text is about 21 px. It scales with the viewport, with a line height of 1.3. Short section labels use the body size. Set commands and technical notes in Geist Mono.

Give the main thought the foreground color. Use the muted tone for context, examples, or a supporting paragraph. Bring the ink forward again when someone needs to make a decision.

Apollo’s dark canvas pairs near-black with soft white. Use the shared color tokens for surfaces, text, and fine rules. Light mode reverses the paper and ink. Match the system appearance on first visit and remember the reader’s choice.

Controls and feedback

Use a link to take someone somewhere and a button to perform an action. Keep field labels visible while typing. When an entry needs correction, keep what was typed and explain the fix beside the field. “Enter a date after 18 November” tells the reader exactly what to change.

Group related actions on one surface, as with the figure arrows above. Give icon controls an accessible name and a 44 px target. Keep keyboard focus visible and convey selection with a persistent cue. Honor reduced motion and reduced transparency preferences.

Account for the whole task. Show progress during a save, prevent duplicate submissions, and announce completion in place. An empty list needs a first action. A failed request needs a way to retry.

Figures and motion

Choose a drawing that explains the subject. A keyboard belongs beside a shortcut list, where the commands can still be selected and copied. Reserve the drawing’s space as the page loads. Describe meaningful imagery, give interactive figures keyboard controls, and provide a usable still view for readers who prefer reduced motion.

Build and review

Copy the system/ folder into your project and load its stylesheet. The fonts, figures, and theme tokens travel together.

Load system/theme.js in the document head to apply the saved appearance. For this article layout, include system/editorial.css, set cxl-editorial on the body, and wrap the prose in cxl-reading. Use cxl-wide-figure when an illustration needs the full width.

Before shipping, complete the principal task with a keyboard. Check both appearances, reduced motion, long labels, phone widths, and 200% zoom. Try the empty, loading, success, and error states.
