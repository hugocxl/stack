Vectors is a library of isometric drawings for websites and apps. Each one shows a specific moment, from a product launch to an empty inbox, in thin lines that take the color of your text.

Drawn for real moments

Every vector has a place on a site in mind: a launch page, a failed payment, a booking that went through. They are grouped by what you want to say, so the right one is quick to find.

Your colors, any size

The lines follow the color of the text around them, so they sit naturally in light and dark themes. They stay thin and sharp, from a small card to a full-width hero.

Wherever you build

Download an SVG, or install one package for JavaScript, React and React Native. A Swift package brings the same drawings to SwiftUI, UIKit and AppKit.

The guide shows how to add them to your project.

Free to use

Use them in personal and commercial work under the MIT license. Keep the license with any copies of the source files.
