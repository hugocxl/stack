CXL Design System

CXL is the design system we use across our websites. It brings the writing, drawings, and controls together in one place.

The keyboard below is one of the drawings we use to explain an interface. Move across its keys, then try the other figures. Each one responds in place, so you can explore it and carry on reading.

Across our projects, Geist Sans carries the whole conversation. We use the same face for an opening passage, a field label, and the button beside it. The passage gets a generous measure and a light weight. The button gets a short verb and a little more ink. A line of Geist Mono marks something you can type, copy, or look up.

Our colors come from Apollo. In dark mode, a near-black canvas carries soft white text, and a quieter gray sets off captions and supporting details. In daylight, we turn the paper light and bring the ink forward. The reading order holds in either appearance.

Copy the system/ folder into your project and load its stylesheet. The fonts, figures, and theme tokens travel together.
