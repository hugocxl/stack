CXL brings our websites together through type, spacing, and a single scale of gray.

Start with what someone came to read or do. Give that thought a heading, set the explanation beneath it, and put the next action within reach.

Writing with type

A change of voice marks a change of purpose. Serif introduces a subject, sans carries the explanation, and mono makes technical details easy to pick out.

Geist Serif
Page and section headings
Geist Sans
Body, controls, and H3–H6
Geist Mono
Code and technical notes

Working in gray

Paper and ink carry the strongest contrast. Use the middle tones to separate surfaces and give supporting details a quieter voice.

Figures and controls

Use a drawing where a reader needs to see how something works. Explore three interactive figures from the bundled Hairline collection.

Start a website

Copy the system/ folder into your project and load its stylesheet. The fonts, figures, and theme tokens travel together.
